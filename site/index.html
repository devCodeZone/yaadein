Yaadein (यादें) organises the photos on your own hard disk, Google Drive and Google Photos, like Google Photos does, except everything stays on your computer.

⬇ Download for your computer Windows macOS Linux

Free & open source · no account · no upload

🧑‍🤝‍🧑People

Finds faces and groups them automatically. Name someone once, then search their name everywhere.

✨Things

Recognises 1,000 objects and scenes (dogs, food, beaches, cars, documents) and groups them.

🔎Search & filters

“riya beach 2023”. Filter by person, thing, year, videos, favourites and location.

🪄Tidy your disk

Copy or move photos into Year/Month, People or Things folders. Preview first, undo any time.

🧹Duplicates

Finds identical copies from WhatsApp, backups and Google and moves them aside. Nothing is deleted.

📱On your phone

Scan a QR code and browse the whole library from your phone over home Wi-Fi.

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Download & install
    The installer isn't code-signed yet. On Windows click “More info → Run anyway”; on a Mac right-click the app → Open.
  2. Add your hard disk or a photo folder
    Sources → Add folder or drive. Google Drive, Google Photos and Takeout exports work too.
  3. Let it learn, then name your people
    Faces and things are found in the background, right on your computer.
